**Run date:** 2026-07-14
**Backend:** Azure AI Foundry, deployment `gpt-5.4` (`LLM_PROVIDER=azure`)
**Framework:** LINDDUN (mapping-table-gated per-flow generation), all three grounding modes
**Scenarios:** KidsTube, Smart Home, Family Location — the three scenarios with a LINDDUN gold
standard. (Genomic is evaluated under PANOPTIC only, per the Week 8 pivot, so it is not in this run.)
**Command:** `python cli.py generate --scenario <s> [--rag|--ungrounded]` then

correctness ordering below is the stable, reproducible finding, not the raw per-category scores.

## Notes on this run

- **Core thesis reproduced exactly:** grounded holds 1.00 citation validity across all three
  scenarios; rag and ungrounded sit below it every time. The ablation's headline claim is
  stable across generation runs.
- **Raw-score wobble is sampling noise.** Most F1s are within ±0.02 of the previous generation.
  This run should not be read as a quality change — the prompt, KB, and model are unchanged from
  the previous generation; only the random sample differs.
- **RAG is the highest-variance mode.** `smart_home rag` recall came in at 0.44 this run (vs. 0.67
  previously) — retrieval and generation are both stochastic, consistent with the Week 8 finding
  that RAG's advantage over ungrounded is scenario-conditional rather than uniform.
- **Family Location grounded is no longer a perfect sweep** (19/20 this run, was 20/20). The
  suspiciously-perfect recall flagged as a likely self-authorship-circularity artifact in
  `WEEK8_REPORT.md` was not robust to re-sampling — supporting the caveat that its numbers should
  not be cited as comparable to KidsTube/Genomic until independently reviewed.

# Genomic PANOPTIC 3-Way Ablation — 2026-07-14

**Backend:** Azure AI Foundry, deployment `gpt-5.4`
**Framework:** MITRE PANOPTIC (no Process-mediation gate — every flow attempted), all three grounding modes
**Scenario:** Genomic Sequencing (NIST SP 1800-43C), the one scenario whose gold standard carries native `panoptic_actions`.
**Matching:** exact PANOPTIC sub-activity id (e.g. `PA03.09`) membership in the gold threat's own `panoptic_actions` list, plus same-flow location.

Regenerated **after** the Week 9 re-OCR correction of the genomic gold standard (see below), so
these score against corrected `panoptic_actions` (ids 63/64/68/69) and fully-resolved DFD locations
(0 unresolved, was 2).

## Scores

| Mode | n_gen (with panoptic_action) | TP | FP | FN | Precision | Recall | F1 | recall (reach-adj) | unresolved |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| grounded | 172 | 19 | 153 | 80 | 0.11 | 0.19 | 0.14 | 0.19 | 0 |
| rag | 100 | 19 | 81 | 80 | 0.19 | 0.19 | 0.19 | 0.19 | 0 |
| ungrounded | 212 | 12 | 200 | 87 | 0.06 | 0.12 | 0.08 | 0.12 | 0 |

PANOPTIC mode has no `structurally_unreachable` concept (every flow is attempted), so every
unmatched gold threat with a resolved location is a genuine recall miss (`reachable_but_missed` =
80 for grounded/rag, 87 for ungrounded).

## Notes on this run

- **grounded > rag > ungrounded holds**, and RAG matches grounded on true positives (19) with far
  fewer false positives (81 vs. 153), giving RAG the best F1 (0.19). Ungrounded is worst on every
  axis, as expected for the ablation baseline.
- **`unresolved_location` is 0 across all three modes** (was 2 in every prior genomic run). The
  Week 9 gold re-OCR resolved the two formerly-unlocatable threats, so the full 99-threat catalog
  is now anchorable.
- **Absolute F1 stays low (0.08–0.19) by construction, not by generation quality.** PANOPTIC scores
  on exact sub-activity id against 100 possible ids (~2.5 accepted per gold threat) — a far stricter
  target than LINDDUN's coarse type+flow tier. A coarse category-level PANOPTIC matching tier (the
  PANOPTIC analogue of LINDDUN's `--strict` split) remains the open follow-up (WEEK8/WEEK9 reports).

## Genomic gold-standard re-OCR (Week 9, this date)

The genomic DFD and gold standard were fully re-parsed against the NIST primary-source figures:
- **Figure 11 (flows, 99 rows):** verified 100% accurate, zero changes.
- **Figures 4–7 (elements):** LIMS (S4-PH) retyped `DataStore → Process` (NIST draws it with the
  Software icon in all three DFDs, like the other two Process-typed software components).
- **Figure 20 (gold threats):** corrected a mis-transcribed de-identification cluster (ids 18–27) —
  2 wrong nodes (24, 25; Figure 24 corroborated the corrections), 8 shifted/phantom descriptions
  (including a "within the data delivery DMZ" threat that appears nowhere in Figure 20), 5 wrong
  scenario_ids, and 4 wrong `panoptic_actions`. With the raw corrected, all 99 gold threats map to
  Figure 11 row = id with matching nodes; every override/best-fit/unresolved workaround was retired.
- **Result:** DFD locations 99/99 resolved, all high-confidence; `verify_genomic.py` 99/99
  corroborated (0 flagged, was 2); LINDDUN reachability 72/27/0.
