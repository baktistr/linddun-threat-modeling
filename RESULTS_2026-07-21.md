## §2 — KidsTube source→DFD adapter (the "DFD mode")

The source→DFD adapter turns the KidsTube repository (`Privacy-Engineering-CMU/KidsTube-PE`, commit
`8e98a1f`) into a DFD, in three arms that mirror the pipeline's own grounded/RAG/ungrounded
ablation one level up. Scored against the hand-authored KidsTube DFD by *provenance key* (never by
element name). Element/flow recall is derivability-adjusted (the 2 planned-feature elements P4/EE3
and their flows DF13/DF14 are structurally underivable from code — the ceiling — and excluded).
`llm`/`llm_naive` are n=3 (Azure `gpt-5.4`); `facts_only` is deterministic.

| Metric | `facts_only` (no LLM) | `llm` (closed fact-id) | `llm_naive` (open file:line) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| n_elements | 14 | 14.0 ±1.7 | 17.0 ±0.0 |
| n_flows | 27 | 110.7 ±4.0 | 45.3 ±5.8 |
| element precision | 0.79 | 0.77 ±0.06 | 0.35 ±0.00 |
| element recall (derivability-adj) | 1.00 | 1.00 ±0.00 | 0.60 ±0.00 |
| flow precision | 0.33 | 0.61 ±0.15 | 0.02 ±0.00 |
| flow recall (derivability-adj) | 0.60 | 0.64 ±0.28 | 0.07 ±0.00 |
| **citation** citations_resolvable | 1.00 | 1.00 ±0.00 | **1.00 ±0.00** |
| **citation** evidence_type_consistent | 1.00 | 1.00 ±0.00 | 0.69 ±0.03 |
| **citation** facts_present (exact line) | 1.00 | 1.00 ±0.00 | 0.27 ±0.03 |
| **citation** all_valid | **1.00** | **1.00 ±0.00** | **0.25 ±0.03** |
| flow evidence_connects | 1.00 | 0.58 ±0.06 | 0.21 ±0.04 |
| ceiling (P4/EE3) held at 2 | ✅ | ✅ every run | ✅ every run |

**The finding — the open citation vocabulary is where citation validity collapses (1.00 → 0.25),
and its decomposition is the argument for fact-id citations:**

- `citations_resolvable = 1.00` — the open `file:line` citations always point at a **real** source
  line. The model never fabricated a file path or an out-of-range line.
- `facts_present = 0.27` — but they land on the *exact* line the extractor pins a construct to only
  27% of the time. So the citations are "real but not deterministically verifiable": near the right
  code, rarely the precise construct, so accepting them would require fuzzy line-matching — which
  reintroduces exactly the judgment the closed fact-id vocabulary removes (`llm`/`facts_only` are
  1.00 by construction).
- The naive arm also over-produces flows (≈45 vs. the hand DFD's 17) and its element/flow quality
  drops sharply (element P 0.35 vs. `llm` 0.77).
- **Nuance vs. the Week 10 prediction:** P4/EE3 did *not* confabulate — the ceiling held at 2 every
  run. The naive model over-decomposed *implemented* code ("MongoDB Database Server",
  "Authorization Middleware") rather than inventing the planned AI engine / advertiser.

`facts_only` (deterministic, no LLM) finds every implemented element and is the bar the LLM arms
must clear; `llm` matches it on elements (0.77 vs 0.79, inside noise) and buys naming and better
flow precision; `llm_naive` is the ablation baseline and underperforms both.

Outputs: `storage/derived/kidstube_{facts_only,llm,llm_naive}_run*.json` (+ `_runs.json`).

## §3 — Threats generated *on* the derived DFD vs. the hand DFD (M4)

`cli.py generate --scenario kidstube_derived` in all three modes, on the committed `facts_only`
derived DFD (14 elements, 27 flows), scored against the hand baseline **restricted to the same
anchorable gold subset**: 27 of 41 gold threats anchor to a flow the derived DFD has; the other 14
(2 planned-feature ceiling flows DF13/DF14 + 6 adapter-miss flows DF4/5/7/8/12/16) are
`unresolved_location` and kept out of the recall denominator, so the comparison is apples-to-apples.
`reach_R` = matched / (matched + reachable_but_missed) on the 27-threat subset.

| Mode | Side | n_gen | matched | reach_R (on 27) | citation all_valid |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| grounded | hand DFD | 118 | 19 | **0.70** | 1.00 |
| grounded | derived DFD | 144 | 14 | **0.52** | 1.00 |
| rag | hand DFD | 82 | 11 | 0.41 | 0.96 |
| rag | derived DFD | 129 | 12 | 0.44 | 0.99 |
| ungrounded | hand DFD | 109 | 16 | 0.59 | 0.92 |
| ungrounded | derived DFD | 161 | 13 | 0.48 | 0.94 |

**Deriving the DFD from source costs recall but not citation validity** — exactly `ABSTRACT.md`'s
claim that traceability's benefit concentrates in citation reliability rather than raw recall. In
grounded mode, recall on the same 27 gold threats falls 0.70 → 0.52 (the coarser `facts_only`
modeling misses ~5 more), while threat-level citation validity is preserved (1.00 → 1.00; every
cited node/type/location still resolves, `location_valid = 1.00` because the derived DFD is a valid
anchor target). The grounding order grounded > ungrounded > rag holds on both DFDs. Precision is
unrestricted and lower on the derived side, which over-produces (144/129/161 vs. 118/82/109).

Outputs: `storage/generated/kidstube_derived_{grounded,rag,ungrounded}.json`,
`storage/generated/kidstube_derived_m4_comparison.txt`.
