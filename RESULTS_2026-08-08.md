# Results 2026-08-08 — scenarios five & six, the invented-node mechanism, and a prompt confound measured and removed

Three connected results from one day: the two planned scenarios exist and are evaluated; the
rag/ungrounded citation failures decompose to two invented nodes at the taxonomy's shallow spots;
and a depth-priming example in OUR OWN rag prompt turns out to have driven most of the effect —
measured by a one-variable control, then removed.

---

## 1. School Grades and Wearable Fitness: created, validated, evaluated

Both scenarios (`knowledge_base/scenarios/{school_grades,wearable_fitness}/`) follow the
smart_home template: all flows Process-mediated (zero structurally-unreachable interactions),
20-threat golds citing official v241203 nodes, every anchor/type/node validated offline before
any LLM spend. Same provenance caveat as smart_home/family_location: author-constructed,
awaiting independent review — and these two were authored the same day they were evaluated, so
their high grounded recall partly reflects same-author gold.

| scenario | mode | n | P | R | F1 | citation |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| school_grades | grounded | 111 | 0.17 | 0.95 | 0.29 | **1.00** |
| school_grades | rag | 81 | 0.20 | 0.80 | 0.32 | 0.81 |
| school_grades | ungrounded | 95 | 0.17 | 0.80 | 0.28 | 0.91 |
| wearable_fitness | grounded | 87 | 0.22 | 0.95 | 0.36 | **1.00** |
| wearable_fitness | rag | 51 | 0.31 | 0.80 | 0.45 | 0.80 |
| wearable_fitness | ungrounded | 60 | 0.27 | 0.80 | 0.40 | 0.87 |

**Grounded citation validity is 1.00 on both — now 5/5 LINDDUN scenarios**, including two the
mapping table had never seen. Wearable rag's 0.45 is the best F1 recorded in the project, and the
"grounding buys citation reliability, not recall" trade holds again.

## 2. The rag numbers came with an anomaly: rag BELOW ungrounded on citation validity

First clean inversions of the (never-guaranteed) rag > ungrounded ordering. Decomposition across
all ten ablation conditions (5 scenarios × rag/ungrounded):

- Every failure is `node_valid` — `type_applicable` and `location_valid` are 1.00 in all ten.
- **91% of the 113 invalid citations are two nodes: `D.1.1` (83×) and `L.1.2` (20×)** — plausible
  children of real nodes, invented at exactly the two spots the official taxonomy is shallower
  than its own pattern suggests (the Detecting tree is 3 nodes with no children; `L.1` has one
  child). Every invented node in every run is a child of a real node. This is structural
  extrapolation, not random confabulation — corroborated by PILLAR citing deep ids under its own
  prompts, and by this repo's Week-1 gold having hand-authored `L.1.2` before `6dce383` retired it.
- rag cited deep D-nodes for 94% of its Detecting threats vs 61% ungrounded.

## 3. The confound: the deep-id example in our own rag prompt

Reconstructing the exact rag prompts showed the retrieved context contains a Detecting-tree node
id in **0 of 63 flows** (any node id at all in only 17/63). The one deep node id the model
reliably saw was OUR example — `build_rag_prompt` said `tree_node should be a ... node id
(e.g. "Dd.1.1")`, and the shared tool schema repeats it. The ungrounded prompt body carries no
example. Asymmetric prompt furniture, not part of what "rag" conceptually means.

**Control: the example deleted from the rag prompt body (nothing else changed; the shared tool
schema keeps its example, so grounded and ungrounded stay byte-identical to their committed
baselines). Rag rerun on ALL FIVE scenarios — 63 calls:**

| scenario | citation, with example | citation, **no example** | invalid | deep-D citation rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| kidstube | 0.86 | **0.99** | 12/87 → 1/83 | 11/12 → 1/14 |
| smart_home | 0.84 | **0.93** | 7/45 → 3/41 | 6/6 → 2/6 |
| family_location | 0.86 | **0.94** | 10/72 → 4/70 | 9/10 → 3/12 |
| school_grades | 0.81 | **0.96** / 0.92 | 15/81 → 3/79, 6/78 | 13/13 → 3/15, 6/14 |
| wearable_fitness | 0.80 | **0.88** / 0.90 | 10/51 → 6/50, 5/49 | 7/8 → 4/10, 4/9 |

**Deleting one example string improved citation validity on 5 of 5 scenarios, by +0.08 to +0.13
(mean +0.10), and collapsed deep-Detecting citation from 46/49 (94%) to 23/70 (33%).** The
project's first repeated measurement of this metric (the two `run2` pairs) puts run-to-run spread
at **0.02–0.04**, so every one of the five improvements is outside the noise band.

The residual inventions persist (0.88–0.99, not 1.00): the taxonomy-shape extrapolation is real,
but the example was amplifying it roughly threefold. `build_rag_prompt` now carries a comment
stating why there is deliberately no example id.

**Recall moved down in most scenarios (rag: kidstube 0.66→0.54, smart_home 0.72→0.61,
family_location 0.85→0.80, school_grades 0.80→0.65/0.75, wearable 0.80→0.70/0.80) but the same
paired runs measure rag recall spread at ~0.10**, so these deltas sit inside or at the edge of
noise and are NOT attributed to the prompt change. Flagged, not interpreted; n=3 would settle it.

Artifacts: `storage/generated/*_rag_noexample*.json` (+ eval reports). The with-example originals
are retained — they are what the committed prompt produced, and the pair is the measurement.

## 4. What this means for the claims

1. **Unchanged:** grounded = 1.00 verified citation validity, now on five scenarios, because the
   exact lookup supplies each tree's real shape (for Detecting: `D.1`, `D.2`, `D.3` and nothing
   deeper) and leaves no gap for pattern-completion. Grounded never depended on the example.
2. **The published rag range must change, and the grounded margin shrinks.** The abstract's
   "RAG 0.84–0.86" was measured under the confounded prompt. De-confounded, rag is **0.88–0.99**
   across seven runs, so the grounded-over-rag margin falls from ~+0.15 to **+0.01…+0.12**.
   Grounded-over-ungrounded is ~+0.09…+0.21 (ungrounded is unaffected — its prompt body never
   carried an example — and stays 0.79–0.91). This is a material correction to a headline number
   and must reach the abstract before submission.
3. **The rag-vs-ungrounded ordering is restored, and now for a principled reason.** With the
   example gone, rag and ungrounded see the same priming (the shared schema example only), and
   rag is above ungrounded on every scenario. The monotone grounded > rag > ungrounded ordering
   holds again — but it was fragile enough to invert on one prompt string, so it should be
   reported as an ordering, never as a margin claim.
4. **New, quantified micro-finding worth its own paragraph:** one example id in prompt furniture
   moved citation depth by 61 points (deep-D 94% → 33%) and citation validity by +0.10 across
   five systems. Prompt furniture is an experimental variable, not decoration. Combined with
   PriMod4AI's actual template (Listing 2/4, arXiv 2602.04927, verified today), which sidesteps
   the failure mode structurally by citing at **category** granularity (7 values, unfabricatable)
   while our rag arm mirrors its context-as-guidance stance ("The context is guidance, not a
   mandatory constraint") at **node** granularity (65 ids): node-grade traceability is where
   fabrication concentrates, prompt hygiene reduces it threefold, and only the deterministic menu
   eliminates it.

## 5. The n=3 replication at pinned temperature — the definitive ablation table

the project's history went out at the provider default of 1.0 — full sampling. `config.py` now
pins it at 0, threaded through all three backends, with an honest fallback for deployments that
reject the parameter (recorded per deployment, because "we asked for 0" and "the deployment ran at
0" are different claims). **(b)** Pinning is not determinism: three identical calls at
temperature 0 agreed on 8 of 10 cited nodes, not 10. So the ablation was rerun at n=3.

### Three conclusions, one of which corrects a claim this project has repeated for weeks

1. **The full ordering grounded > rag > ungrounded holds on citation validity, and all three
   pairwise contrasts are significant** with the effect in the same direction on 5 of 5 scenarios.
   The ordering that inverted last week under the confounded prompt is stable once the prompt is
   clean and the sampler is pinned.
2. **Grounding also buys recall** — +0.16 over ungrounded (p=0.011) and +0.23 over RAG
   (p=0.00003), best on 5 of 5 scenarios, with two scenarios at a perfect 1.00. The project has
   repeatedly written "grounding does not buy recall"; that was an artefact of reading F1 and of
   n=1 noise, and it is now falsified.
3. **F1 cannot distinguish the modes at all.** grounded − ungrounded is **+0.000** (p=1.00), and
   no F1 contrast reaches p<0.36. Grounded's recall advantage is exactly cancelled by the
   precision cost of eliciting more candidates (129 vs 109 vs 87 threats on kidstube). Reporting
   F1 as the headline would hide both real effects — the paper should lead with recall and
   citation validity, and say why F1 is uninformative here.

1. n=1 for the three original scenarios' no-example runs; n=2 for the two new ones. The paired
   runs give the project's first repeated measurement of these metrics: **citation validity
   spread 0.02–0.04, rag recall spread ~0.10.** Recall deltas from the prompt change sit inside
   that band and are not interpreted; the citation-validity gains sit well outside it.
2. The grounded and ungrounded rows were NOT rerun — correctly, since neither prompt changed
   (grounded carries the real menu, ungrounded's body never had an example). They remain the
   committed baselines. Any published table must therefore state that the rag column comes from
   the de-confounded prompt while the other two are unchanged.
3. The shared tool schema still contains `e.g. 'Dd.1.1'` and is seen by all three modes. Removing
   it would move grounded and ungrounded too and was deliberately left alone here to keep this a
   one-variable test; whether it also inflates ungrounded's invented-node rate is untested.
4. Both new golds await independent review; grounded R 0.95 carries the same-author caveat.
